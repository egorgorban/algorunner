<objective>
Two slices on top of 03-01's proven fan-out, both about the stage that decides and runs the branches.

1. **Curation (STRAT-02, D-01..D-03).** The Strategist proposes, then selects at most `max_approaches` (default 3) approaches worth teaching, in one call. Each carries a role label and a "why included" rationale. The node enforces the cap and drops duplicates deterministically. Role and rationale become part of the Approach contract here; Plan 03-04 feeds them to the Editorial Writer as input only, and the Writer still owns order and bridges (D-04).
2. **Branch hardening (D-05, D-06, D-09, Pitfalls 1-3).** Prove on whole-graph, multi-branch runs that a crashing branch never takes its siblings down, the join routes once, zero verified approaches fail with the precedence error, and each branch has its own correction budget. Make re-invocation idempotent with the Strategist's `Overwrite({})` reset.

Purpose: ROADMAP SC1 (curated multiple approaches) and the guarantee that a crashing or exhausted approach never costs its siblings their verified work.
Output: the curation contract, prompt and guard, the outcome reset, and `tests/graph/test_fan_out.py`.
</objective>
