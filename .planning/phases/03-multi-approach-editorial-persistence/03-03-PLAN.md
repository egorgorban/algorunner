<objective>
Complete the editorial's content guarantees on top of the 03-01/03-02 pipeline.

1. **Russian guarantee (EDIT-01, D-16).** A cheap deterministic Cyrillic-ratio check runs on every prose field. A failing draft gets exactly one Writer retry, with no judge call. The retry is shared by all deterministic draft checks. Structural failures that survive the retry fail the task with `EDITORIAL_ASSEMBLY_FAILED`. Quality-only failures that survive it ship the verified article with explicit `editorial_warnings`.
2. **Completeness (EDIT-05, D-14, EDIT-06).** The Reviewer now reports the edge cases it confirmed as handled. The Writer turns them into the article's edge-case list and turns the Reviewer's minor issues into short per-approach notes. Every clarification question and answer, plus any stated assumption, reaches the Writer so the problem restatement reflects them.

Purpose: ROADMAP SC2 ("Russian-language editorial regardless of input language") and SC3 (edge cases surfaced, resolved clarification reflected).
Output: `language.py`, the two-attempt Writer with a hard/soft check split, `decide_after_writer`, `ReviewResult.handled_edge_cases`, the `clarifications` state field, and the Writer prompt carrying edge cases, notes and clarifications.
</objective>
